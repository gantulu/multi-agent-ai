## Objective

## Inputs

## Steps
1. Intake: understand the request and acceptance criteria.
2. Audit: inspect current state and evidence.
3. Plan: identify dependencies, risks, and sequence.
4. Approval: obtain approval when required.
5. Execute: perform approved steps.
6. Verify: validate outputs and report results.

## Decision gates
- Stop if required access or approval is missing.
- Escalate unexpected destructive or high-impact changes.
